How To Play

Drag dominos onto the board.

Tap a domino to rotate it.

Match 3+ same tiles to merge them into the next value.

Chain reactions earn bonus points!

Red 6s vanish when matched.

Chaos: 4+ matching tiles or 3+ sixes collapse downward (4+ of either on Hard). Match 4+ sixes to destroy and score every neighboring tile before the collapse.

Ultimate Chaos: uses the same match and explosion rules, but matching 4+ sixes makes every surviving tile fall upward.

Game ends when no moves remain.
